## 6. Key gotchas & traps (⚠️ READ before touching anything)

1. **Sentinel trap** — `logs/_last_swing_date.txt` is **tracked in git** and committed by bot runs. It contains the date of the last full swing scan (today: `2026-08-05`). Same-day swing runs **skip the full scan** (exits only). To force a re-scan: manually reset it to yesterday (`2026-08-04`) and push, or the bot rewrites it after a full scan.
2. **Breakeven rule** — P&L=0 close is **neither win nor loss**. Use wins+losses for win rate denominator.
3. **Duplicate guard** — same ticker + same direction already open → entry SKIPPED + logged with reason. (Entry limits were removed in `f25b4fc`; only `MAX_CONCURRENT=100` total + duplicate guard remain.)
4. **Gap-down signals** in the `fired_patterns` loop: they lack a `fired` key → safely skipped. Don't "fix" this.
5. **Version discipline** — canonical = **v5.10**. Don't introduce per-file version strings.
6. **TG message limits** — `MAX_OPEN_PER_PAGE=3`, `MAX_CLOSED_PER_PAGE=5`, `TG_CHAR_LIMIT=4000` (bot.py:156-158). Paginates.
7. **`Data 39/54 ok`** is informational (US closed → partial ticker data). Not a bug. ⚠️ icon only when `errors>0`.
8. **portfolio.json is STRICT JSON** — any `NaN` from pandas must be cleaned (a663969 pattern: `_clean_nan` before save).
9. **Run before 1 IST** — `00 01 * * *` cron is the daily full swing scan (00:00 UTC = 05:30 IST). Tomorrow (2026-08-06) is the **first full swing scan with v5.10 code** — verify its logs.
10. **No exchange-holiday guard** — "Market Closed" text is informational only; scans still run.
